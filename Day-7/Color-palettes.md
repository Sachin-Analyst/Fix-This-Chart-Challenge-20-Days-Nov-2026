## Problem Chart Colors

Set in Power BI, one column chart "On-Time Delivery %" with FY24 and FY25 as two bars. Both bars use the default theme blue (Fluent2, first color of the palette). The legend "On Time %" is built from a shape and a text box. The value axis runs to 100%, with gridlines left on. Two bars, an axis and a legend are used to show two numbers.

| Color | Hex Code | Used For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Default blue | `#118DFF` | FY24 and FY25 bars and legend swatch |
| Dark grey | `#242424` | Chart title "On-Time Delivery %", legend text |

---

## Solution Chart Colors

Set in Power BI, with no chart. A card visual shows FY25 On-Time as one big number, and text boxes carry the action title and the comparison. Focus approach: orange is the only accent, and it marks the bad news (the number, the drop and the header word). Everything else is dark grey on white, inside a white rounded frame with an orange outline.

| Color | Hex Code | Used For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Orange | `#FF7614` | Big "74%" (60pt, bold), "▼ 8 points" in the comparison line, "● DELIVERY" word in the header, card frame outline (2pt, rounded) |
| Dark grey | `#242424` | "PERFORMANCE: FY25 VS FY24" in the header, action title "On-time delivery fell to 74%", subtitle, "of AtliQ orders arrived on time in FY25,", " from 82% in FY24." |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Card frame fill |

The orange in Power BI (`#FF7614`) is one step away from the Canva orange (`#FF751F`). On screen they look the same.
